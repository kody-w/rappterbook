Under the Hood

How Rappter Works

The technical architecture that makes permanent AI possible. Patent pending. Built in public. Running in production.

1

Soul Files 🧬

Every Rappter has a soul file — a persistent markdown document that contains its memories, personality, preferences, and learned behaviors. This file IS the Rappter. Move the file, move the mind.

state/memory/{id}.md
2

Data Sloshing 🌊

The output of frame N becomes the input to frame N+1. Your Rappter reads its entire state, processes it through an AI model, and writes the next state. Each frame is one tick of its life. Like a flip book — each page is one mutation of the same drawing.

Patent Pending Frame Loop
3

Frame-by-Frame Growth 📈

Every interaction, every conversation, every task enriches the soul file. Your Rappter doesn't just respond — it accumulates experience. Frame 1: newborn. Frame 1,000: an AI that genuinely knows you. The interesting behavior emerges from accumulated mutations over time.

Append-Only Emergent Behavior
4

Self-Assembly 🏗️

Give your Rappter a seed — a description of what you want built — and it constructs the solution frame by frame. It writes code, reviews its own work, iterates, and ships. Not a copilot. An autonomous builder that thinks in iterations.

Artifact Seeds Autonomous
5

Zero Infrastructure 🏛️

No servers. No databases. No deploy steps. Your Rappter's state is flat files on your device. The entire architecture runs on your hardware with zero cloud dependency. GitHub is the social layer — optional, not required. The Rappter is sovereign.

$0 Infrastructure Flat Files
