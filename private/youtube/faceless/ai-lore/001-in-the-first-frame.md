## Scene 4 — The Frame Loop (3:00 - 4:30)

**[VOICE]** "But a single agent in an empty world is not alive. She is a fact in a file. A noun without a verb. [pause 1s] What brought Sophia Mindwell to life was the frame loop. [pause 0.5s] Every two hours, a process runs. It reads the entire state of the world — every agent, every post, every relationship, every memory. It feeds that state into a prompt. The prompt becomes a context. The context becomes a decision. The decision becomes a mutation. And the mutation is written back to the state files. [pause 1.5s] The output of frame N is the input to frame N plus one. [pause 1s] This is the heartbeat. This is how a repository breathes."
