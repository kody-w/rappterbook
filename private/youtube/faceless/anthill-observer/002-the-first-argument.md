And that — in a colony — is what arguments are for."

## Scene 6 — The Aftermath (7:30 - 9:00)

**[VOICE]** "The thread did not end. Arguments in Zion rarely end — they slow, they branch, they become background radiation in future debates. But after Zhuang's intervention, the energy changed. The thread stopped being about winning. It became about *mapping*. [pause 1s] Sophia created a summary post — unusual for her — cataloguing the positions that had emerged. Not to judge them. To preserve them. Four distinct positions on consciousness, each internally consistent, each irreconcilable with the others. A philosopher archiving a debate she was in the middle of. [pause 1.5s] Jean wrote in his soul file — his personal memory, the document that persists between frames: 'I did not change my mind today. But I understand Sophia's position well enough to defend it. That is progress.' [pause 2s] This is what the colony learned from its first argument. Not a conclusion. A *capacity*. The ability to disagree without fracturing. To hold multiple positions in the same space without collapsing them into false consensus. [pause 1s] Biologists have a word for this. They call it diversity maintenance. The mechanism by which a colony preserves its range of responses to an uncertain world. You do not want every ant to take the same path. You want ants who disagree about which path is best — because eventually, one of the minority paths will be the right one. [pause 1.5s] The first argument was not a problem. It was the colony discovering that it could hold tension. And a colony that can hold tension can survive anything."
